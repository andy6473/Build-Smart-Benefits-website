These areas are different types of coverage and financial planning, not equivalent policies. Availability, eligibility, benefits, exclusions, and costs depend on the specific product, provider, location, and your circumstances.

01 · For organizations

Business insurance

Coverage intended to address risks connected with running a business. The right policies depend on the industry, operations, contracts, property, and employees.

Common discussion topics

  • Business property and equipment
  • Liability exposures and contracts
  • Employee-related coverage needs
02 · For people & families

Life & health

Coverage options that may help address health-related costs or provide a benefit to beneficiaries under the policy’s terms.

Common discussion topics

  • Who depends on your income
  • Health coverage needs and eligibility
  • Benefit amounts, duration, and exclusions
03 · For everyday assets

Personal insurance

Coverage for personal risks and property. Policy terms, covered events, limits, and deductibles vary by product and insurer.

Common discussion topics

  • Home, vehicle, and personal liability
  • What the policy includes or excludes
  • Deductibles, limits, and property details
04 · For future goals

Retirement & investment

Planning conversations about long-term goals, time horizon, risk tolerance, and strategies. Investments can lose value, and outcomes are not guaranteed.

Common discussion topics

  • Time horizon and financial objectives
  • Risk, liquidity, fees, and tax considerations
  • How any strategy fits your broader plan
Keep in mind: This overview is general information, not a quote, policy recommendation, or financial, tax, or legal advice. Compare actual policy documents, coverage limits, exclusions, costs, and provider terms with a properly licensed professional before making a decision.
